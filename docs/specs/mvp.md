# MVP specification

CareerRadar is a single-user Go application for collecting publicly accessible IT opportunities without LLMs. A shared application service supports a Russian web interface and Telegram. PostgreSQL is the source of truth. Sources require user confirmation before monitoring; failures and missing information are visible and never constitute evidence of closure. Store evidence URLs and only facts actually extracted. Deduplicate conservatively, retain change history and suppress repeated unchanged notifications.

Acceptance: source creation, discovery and confirmation, editing, disabling, deletion and CSV import; bounded monitoring and global discovery; evidence-based extraction, pagination, filtering and uncertainty; durable history, idempotency and confirmed closure; daily 08:00 Europe/Moscow scheduling; Telegram summaries, source management, monitoring, application statuses, warnings and one-time expiring account linking; responsive web UI with equivalent shared data, catalog, preferences and check history; documented REST API; local fixture, PostgreSQL, API, integration, race and real-source smoke checks. Parse schema.org JobPosting and EducationalOccupationalProgram JSON-LD and Microdata when present, prefer JSON-LD if both are published, and never infer missing fields from unstructured text. Do not bypass access controls. Missing fields remain unknown, and unchanged warnings are deduplicated.

Security: the personal API must not be public without authentication; source fetching must not grant access to private network targets or follow unsafe redirects. Secrets stay in environment variables. Telegram linking is one-use, expires, and cannot silently replace an existing link.

Cross-source identity: retain persistent aliases only for verified individual Greenhouse, Lever and Ashby posting paths. Generic forms or similar titles do not establish identity. Keep both records and report attention when existing aliases conflict. Preserve source evidence, history and application status across incomplete rechecks.

Pay normalization: accept explicit MONTH and YEAR salary units from schema.org JSON-LD and Microdata. Convert annual pay to monthly equivalents with outward rounding to whole currency units; retain fractional equivalents as ranges. Do not infer monthly pay from hourly or weekly amounts without a published workload.

Global discovery uses public Habr Career RSS search independently of monitored sites. Remotive is removed at the owner's request. Retain attributed links, RSS author and description, literal query relevance, and explicit remote markers. Enrich up to 20 results from individual cards using at most two workers and a 20-second search budget; retain earlier confirmed data and flag failed or incomplete cards. Do not infer missing geography or workload. Reject invalid feed documents and non-vacancy URLs. New companies still require confirmation before monitoring.

Individual Habr vacancy cards use JobPosting JSON-LD. Normalize that provider's city Text labels and modern ruble RUR label; do not apply these source-specific assumptions to arbitrary sites. Preserve known alternative locations in revision descriptions, flag ambiguous locations for attention, and stop related-vacancy traversal on an individual Habr card.
